# Design and creation notes

## Editorial intent

The theme prioritizes a readable argument: a clear title, a useful subtitle, a compact byline, and a body with enough room for evidence. The finance edition is a demonstration of a reusable visual system, not a dashboard or a recommendation engine.

## Hierarchy

- Home establishes the publication, then offers three curated essays and a chronological recent-writing list.
- Articles uses a compact list, topic filters, and a quiet side column.
- Notes uses a date-led rhythm for shorter pieces.
- Article pages give the contents navigation its own column, outside the reading measure.
- About explains editorial scope and practice without fictitious professional achievements.
- Search indexes the shipped content locally. An empty query invites exploration; unmatched queries offer a recovery action.

## Reading rhythm

The article baseline is 16px with 1.65 line-height and 10px paragraph spacing. Titles use local serif faces. Rules and color changes do more work than shadows. Sage, walnut, and warm gray appear in figures and code so that technical content feels part of the page.

Code has an internal height limit of 420px on desktop and 340px on mobile. Wide tables and code scroll within their containers. Images preserve aspect ratio; explicit dimensions reserve space and prevent lazy loading from moving section anchors.

## Content and visual provenance

The sample publication is fictional. Four long-form essays and two notes were created for this template with AI assistance. Financial calculations were checked against the assumptions stated in the text. They do not report market prices or returns. The Northbank Supply business is fictional.

The three data diagrams are original SVG figures. Two-asset volatility uses equal weights, equal 20% asset volatility, and stated correlations. The bond example discounts five annual $40 coupons and $1,000 of principal. The cash-flow bridge is a simplified fictional statement reconciliation.

The architectural hero originated in AI-assisted theme development. It supplies light, structure, and material texture without implying a real office or author identity. Screenshots are captured from the running implementation.

## Accessibility and adaptation

Semantic headings, descriptive image alternatives, keyboard focus styles, a skip link, a keyboard-operable mobile menu, and collapsible mobile contents are included. System fonts avoid remote font requests. Automated tests and browser checks are useful checks, not a claim of formal accessibility certification.

Change text before changing spacing: a new publication name, substantially longer titles, or a different writing language should be checked at both desktop and phone widths. Keep chart units and assumptions readable. Replace decorative images without introducing meaningful data into empty-alt artwork.
